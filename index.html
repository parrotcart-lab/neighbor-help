Neighbor Help

Help at home, from people your neighbors trust. For iPhone and iPad.

See which helpers people in your ZIP code hired, what they paid, and whether they would hire them again. Then ask up to three helpers for a quote with one ready-written message.

What it does

  • Lists helpers by how many neighbors hired them. Nobody can pay to rank higher
  • Shows the price range neighbors paid, before you call anyone
  • Writes your request and opens it as a text to each helper you pick, from your own number
  • Lines up the quotes you get back against what neighbors paid
  • Keeps your own list of who you hired, what it cost and when a job is due again
  • Asks three quick questions after a job, which you can share with neighbors without your name
